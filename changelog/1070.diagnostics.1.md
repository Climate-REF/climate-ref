`ilamb/mrsos-wangmao`, `ilamb/thetao-woa2023-surface` and `ilamb/so-woa2023-surface` (version unchanged): Where a model publishes several of the accepted variables, the execution now only takes the one the diagnostic uses (the top 10 cm `mrsos`, the surface fields `tos` and `sos`). Filtering this at solve level rather than during execution removes the need to rerun executions when unused data changes. CMIP7 soil moisture executions that failed when a model published both `mrsol` variants now succeed.
