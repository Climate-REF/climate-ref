Fixed the CMIP6-to-CMIP7 conversion rounding values to three decimal places, which turned small magnitudes such as precipitation in kg m-2 s-1 into zeros. Converted data is now quantised to five significant digits, and cached conversions with the old rounding are rebuilt.
Fixed ILAMB failing on CMIP7 soil moisture when both `mrsol` variants (top 10 cm and all layers) are present; it now uses the variant the diagnostic is defined for.
Fixed the cloud scatterplots, regional historical annual cycle and time series, and PMP ENSO diagnostics finding no or incomplete CMIP7 inputs when a model publishes their variables on different grids.
