Fixed `ilamb/mrsos-wangmao`, `ilamb/snc-esacci` and `ilamb/amoc-rapid`, none of which could
complete over the full CMIP6 archive.

`ilamb3` is now required at `>=2026.6.15`. Earlier versions derived time measures assuming
`timedelta64[ns]`, so `cftime` differences (any non-standard calendar) came out 1000x too small
and comparability aborted with `dt_ref=0.0 vs dt_com=30.4`. This also retires the `xarray<2025.11`
and `scipy<1.16` pins, which existed only for `ilamb3` issues the new release resolves.

`amoc-rapid` additionally needed a `climate_ref_msftmz_to_rapid` transform: ILAMB's
`msftmz_to_rapid` selects the Atlantic with a hardcoded `isel(basin=0)`, but the `RAPID-2023-1a`
reference has no `basin` dimension.
