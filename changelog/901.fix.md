The PMP provider now installs `pcmdi_metrics` from a fork of 4.2.0 that chunks the SVD in the variability modes analysis.
This cuts the runtime of the extratropical modes of variability diagnostics by roughly 40x.
The jump from 3.9.2 also corrects the modes of variability linear regression, the spatial statistic masking
and the latitude ordering, so the variability modes metrics and the annual cycle `ta` metrics shift.
