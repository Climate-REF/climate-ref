Adds `experiment_id` to the group-by of the ESMValTool diagnostics that pin a single historical experiment.
The metric values they produce now carry the experiment as a dimension,
so historical and `esm-hist` results can be told apart.
The affected diagnostics have their version bumped,
so the next solve creates fresh execution groups under the new keys
and the old ones drop out of the default views.
ECS, TCR, TCRE, ZEC and the warming-level diagnostics already carry it,
because the experiment is fixed by what they measure.
ECS and ENSO characteristics now also declare `variant_label` in their facets,
matching how the CMIP7 requirement groups the data.
