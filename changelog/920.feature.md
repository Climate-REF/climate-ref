Observational data requirements are now served from `obs4REF` only when they declare it as a fallback source type.
The solver previously folded the whole `obs4REF` catalog into `obs4MIPs` before solving, so every requirement received it whether it asked or not.
All in-repo diagnostics declare the fallback, so their behaviour is unchanged.
A custom diagnostic that relied on the old implicit behaviour needs to add `fallback_source_types=(SourceDatasetType.obs4REF,)` to its requirement.
