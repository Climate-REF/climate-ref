Fixed CMIP7 conversion of datasets extended past the end of their CMIP6 source
(`extend_historical_to`). The source file is opened chunked, which makes its `time_bnds` a
Dask array of `cftime` objects, and concatenating the repeated years onto it failed with
`Can not use auto rechunking with object dtype`. `repeat_final_year_to` now materialises the
bounds first.

Converted CMIP7 files now record the version of the CMIP6 dataset they were converted from,
as a `cmip6_version` attribute that the complete parser carries through to the catalog. The
`version` a converted file is published under is the conversion's own -- and
`extend_historical_to` moves it again for fabricated output -- so pinning a CMIP7 test case
to its catalog could previously pick up a newer version of the CMIP6 source. Cached
conversions that do not record the version being converted are rebuilt.
