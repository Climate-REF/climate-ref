`DiagnosticFilter`, `ExecutionGroupFilter` and the execution statistics readers now accept exact `diagnostic_slug` and `provider_slug` matches, alongside the existing `diagnostic_contains`/`provider_contains` substring matches.
Every reader filter now shares the same diagnostic and provider fields.
`InputDatasetFilter` now accepts `dataset_ids` to keep only executions that ran against one of those datasets, and `source_types` may be left empty when `dataset_ids` is set.
