Every reader filter now lives in `climate_ref.results.filters` and is built from a `BaseFilter` plus one mixin per axis: `DiagnosticScope`, `VersionScope`, `ExecutionScope` and `InputDatasetScope`.
A `*Filter` is what you pass to a reader, and a `*Scope` is one axis mixed into it.
A field now means the same thing, with the same default, on every filter that has it.
`DiagnosticFilter` and `ExecutionGroupFilter` now accept exact `diagnostic_slug` and `provider_slug` matches, alongside the `diagnostic_contains`/`provider_contains` substring matches.
`ExecutionScope` gains `created_after` and `created_before`, which apply before each group's latest execution is picked.
Every filter now has every scope, so `ExecutionGroupFilter` and `DiagnosticFilter` accept `include_retracted`, `created_after` and `created_before`, and `DiagnosticFilter` accepts `include_superseded`.
Groups, statistics, diagnostic counts and values now decide which groups count in one place.
`InputDatasetFilter` now accepts `dataset_ids` to keep only executions that ran against one of those datasets, and `source_types` may be left empty when `dataset_ids` is set.
Every filter gains `diagnostic_version`, to read the results of one diagnostic version, promoted or not.
