This release reworks the results reader used by the CLI and the REF app.
Results can now be filtered by the data that went into them, such as a model, a MIP era or specific datasets,
and every filter accepts the same fields with the same defaults.
Common reads are much faster on large databases.
It also adds downloads of published CMIP7 data from ESGF.
